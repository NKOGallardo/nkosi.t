Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services

Do you offer package discounts for multiple bookings?

Yes! We offer package discounts when booking multiple services. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your needs.

What is your turnaround time for edited images?

Standard turnaround is 2-3 weeks for photo editing and 3-4 weeks for video projects. Rush processing available for additional fee.

Do you provide digital copies or prints?

All packages include high-resolution digital copies for printing. Physical prints available through our lab at additional cost.

Can you work with my existing photos or footage?

Absolutely! We offer professional editing and post-production services for images and videos from any source.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer, credit cards, EFT, and cash. A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking.

Do you have a backup photographer in case of emergency?

Yes, we maintain a network of professional backup photographers to ensure your event is covered under any circumstances.

Can I use the images commercially?

Commercial usage rights are included in Premium packages. Contact us to add commercial licensing to other packages.

What if I'm not happy with the final product?

We offer unlimited revisions until you're completely satisfied with the final product. Your satisfaction is our priority.
